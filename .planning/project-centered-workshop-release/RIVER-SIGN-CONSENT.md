## What this session is

We are testing Digital Workshop, not you. You will use three project-provided
River Sign designs to create and prepare a simulated CNC project. No physical
CNC will be connected or commanded. The session includes seven tasks, brief
questions after each task, and three closing questions. You may decline to
answer a question, pause, or stop at any time without penalty.

The moderator cannot teach the interface or tell you where controls are during
the scored tasks. Confusion and unsuccessful tasks are useful study results.

## Data collected and local handling

The study records an anonymous cohort slot and attempt ID, eligibility answers,
task actions and times, mistakes, answers, ease ratings, and closing comments.
It does not require your name in the scored results. This signed form and any
optional recording remain in the facilitator's private local study archive.
Only de-identified task results and aggregate pass/fail totals may enter release
evidence. The facilitator will not publish your name, contact details, voice, or
image.

You may ask to withdraw your attempt by giving the facilitator your attempt
evidence ID any time before the cohort release decision is signed. The
facilitator will delete your private recording and worksheet, mark the attempt
`WITHDRAWN`, retain only a non-identifying withdrawal audit entry, and recruit a
replacement. After identifiers are destroyed and results are aggregated, an
individual result may no longer be linkable for removal.
